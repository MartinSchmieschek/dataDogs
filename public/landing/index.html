chapter 01

what is it

SlopDogs

Your AI writes.SlopDogs runs.

Dogs are small blocks of code your AI writes; a kennel is what it assembles from them. SlopDogs keeps the kennel running, live, at a public URL.

dog+dog+dog→kennel→/k/<name>
